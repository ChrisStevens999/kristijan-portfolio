# DECISION LOG

## Purpose

This document records important decisions made during the development of the portfolio.

The goal is not to document every change.

The goal is to preserve the reasoning behind significant creative, UX, technical and strategic decisions.

Future decisions should reference this log before changing existing systems.

---

## Format

Date

Decision

Reason

Result (optional)

# 2026-07-16

## Decision

The homepage follows this structure:

Intro

↓

Selected Works

↓

Explore by Category

↓

About

↓

Contact

### Reason

The experience should introduce emotion first, showcase the strongest work second, provide exploration third, then introduce the designer before offering contact information.

This creates a narrative rather than a traditional portfolio layout.

# 2026-07-16

## Decision

The homepage begins with an immersive intro sequence.

### Reason

The intro establishes mood and identity before presenting work.

The visitor should remember the experience before remembering individual projects.

# 2026-07-16

## Decision

Selected Works is a curated collection rather than a complete archive.

### Reason

Quality creates stronger perception than quantity.

The homepage should always represent the best work available at that time.

# 2026-07-16

Categories function as chapter covers instead of portfolio filters.

### Reason

The goal is exploration.

# 2026-07-16

### Reason

# 2026-07-16

# 2026-08-26

## Decision

three.js (via react-three-fiber) is an accepted dependency for object-based showcases: the Sticker Archive pole, and later the Apparel garments and the Lab statue.

### Reason

Some work is best shown as a physical object the visitor can turn, not as a flat image. Models are compressed offline by scripts in /scripts and loaded only on the pages that need them, so the rest of the site stays light.

---

# 2026-08-30

## Decision

Project pages are bespoke. Each case study is its own component, built to the reference board supplied for that project, instead of one generic Hero → Challenge → Process → Outcome → Gallery template.

### Reason

The supplied boards differ in structure per project; a single template would have flattened them. 05_COMPONENT_LIBRARY.md allows departing from the standard structure for a compelling reason, and this is it.

### Result

The generic project route renders only a title-only placeholder for projects without a case study (currently Neo Tokyo and Hardcore Mentality). Every project page still ends with navigation to the next project or back to its category.

# 2026-09-10

## Decision

The homepage snaps one gesture per section: the document snaps to the top of every section, and each Selected Work stop is mandatory. The "Selected Work" chapter card is a real stop and no longer auto-advances.

### Reason

Each featured image should be seen on its own, the way the reference site (poison.studio) paces its work. The chapter card was barely seen when it scrolled itself away.

# 2026-10-07

## Decision

About returns to the homepage as a wordless stop between Selected Works and Explore by Category: a deck of ten black-and-white photographs swings open from one card into a floating cluster on arrival and, as the visitor scrolls through a five-screen section (one snap stop per screen, the last two holding on the video and the line), gathers and swings through edge-on and what swings back out is the portrait video, which grows and loops. One line over the video’s corner — “Click to find out about me”, Inter Bold — opens the About page.

### Reason

Requested with supplied photographs and a reference clip. The person behind the work is introduced after the strongest work and before the chapters, as a moment rather than a bio block.

### Result

The homepage order is Intro → Selected Works → About → Explore by Category (→ Contact, planned). /about shows the five supplied boards as artwork for now, one snap stop each; their text becomes live HTML when the page is built out.

## 2026-10-07 — About opening refinement

Refine the supplied-reference opening into a compact, upright photographic
cluster: one centered card turns nearly edge-on, the layers separate in depth,
then spread as the whole deck turns back toward the viewer. Keep individual
cards parallel, balance depth around the scene center, and size the complete
composition to retain black space on phones and desktops. Use subtle shared
rotation rather than independently tilted and bobbing photographs. Preserve
the scroll-controlled gather, red video reveal, CTA, and homepage order.

Reason: the previous large, tilted foreground cards obscured the other images
and read as a scattered pile rather than the reference's spatial arrangement.

Follow-up: the user requested the entire sequence follow scrolling. Start
with one image and no automatic opening. The first scroll screen unfolds
the cards; the next turns the floating cluster; two screens slowly gather
the cards into the video, grow it, and reveal the text last. One final screen
holds the result. All stages reverse with scrolling. There are six stops.
