# TYPOGRAPHY

## Philosophy

Typography is part of the visual identity.

Every font has a specific purpose.

Never substitute these fonts.

If a font cannot be loaded, stop and ask.

---

# Aku Kamu

Purpose

Intro only.

Used for:

- Main intro title
- Hero statement
- Large artistic moments

Avoid using it for navigation, buttons or body copy.

---

# Integral CF

Primary UI font.

Used for:

- Navigation
- Main headings
- Project titles
- About section
- Body copy

This is the portfolio's primary typeface.

---

# Benzin

Accent font.

Used sparingly for:

- Category names
- Small labels
- Metadata
- Dates
- Section identifiers

Never use Benzin for long paragraphs.

---

# Anton

Condensed display face, loaded through next/font/google only on the pages
that use it:

- The Apparel display's name tag (the shirt, pattern or hoodie name)
- The Contact page's poster title (CONTACT ME) and its full-screen menu

Not for anything else.

---

# Inter

The Contact page's grotesque (added 2026-11-11), weights 700 and 800: its
top menu, the form's labels and fields, and the Submit button, as in the
mockup. Loaded through next/font/google on that page only.

Not for headings or long copy elsewhere.

---

# Rules

Typography should remain HTML.

Never bake text into images. The exception is supplied artwork shown as
artwork — the About book’s pages and the case-study panels arrive as finished,
art-directed images with their words in them; their text is repeated as
screen-reader text or alt text.

Maintain generous spacing.

Use hierarchy through size and weight rather than excessive font changes.

Keep the experience editorial and restrained.
